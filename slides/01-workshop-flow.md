# From classroom to first client

A four-step path. Each step builds on the one before.

| Step | Title | One line | Deck part |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resume | One page, numbers not adjectives, a live link | Part 1 |
| 2 | DSA patterns | About 15 patterns, revisited, beat raw problem count | Part 2 |
| 3 | Ship a project | Pick a real annoyance, scope it to a weekend, deploy it | Part 3 |
| 4 | First client | Start with your network, pitch their problem, ask for a testimonial | Part 4 |

Optional band (bottom): **Mock interviews** for anyone who wants practice. Opt in, no pressure.

**Speaker notes:** Use this as the map for the session and as the discussion slide at the end.
Every number on the card already appears later in the deck (about 15 patterns, 7 seconds, weekend scope).
Do not mention days or the tutoring marketplace here.
