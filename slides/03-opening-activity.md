# Pick your corner

**Which one do you most want help with today?**

| Corner | Topic |
|---|---|
| Front left | Resume |
| Front right | DSA patterns |
| Back left | Building a project |
| Back right | Getting a first client |

## Run it (6 minutes)
1. **Set up (30 s).** Point to the four corners. Say: "Stand in the corner for the thing you're most stuck on. There is no wrong answer."
2. **Move (1 min).** Everyone walks. Let them settle.
3. **Talk (2 min).** In each corner, groups of 3 to 4 answer one question: "What have you tried so far, and what went wrong?"
4. **Share (2 min).** Ask one person per corner for one sentence. Write the corners' headcounts on the board.
5. **Close (30 s).** Say: "Today is in this order. Watch for your corner." Then go to the flow slide.

## Why it works
- Gets 30 to 60 people talking in the first five minutes, with no tech.
- Tells you which part to spend more time on, live.
- Seeds the follow-up: "who was in the Resume corner?" can anchor an example later.

## If the room is packed or you are online
- **Packed:** raise hands for each topic instead of walking; count roughly.
- **Online:** a quick poll in chat with the same four options; ask three people to unmute.

## Optional second round (3 minutes, tie-in to the "7 seconds" slide)
Pairs swap a resume or LinkedIn on a phone. The partner looks for 7 seconds, then says the one thing that stood out. Reuse this later when you reach "What a recruiter actually scans for".

**Speaker notes:** Do not ask anyone to share grades or personal details. If a corner is empty, say so and move on; it still tells you something.
